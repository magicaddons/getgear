GetGear v0.14.0 Beta 7 — PRIVATE TEST CANDIDATE, NOT A PUBLIC RELEASE
========================================================================

This is the historical development-data reconciliation checklist for the
Beta 4 to Beta 7 class-equippable catalogue migration.

IMPORTANT
- A candidate appearing in the equipment list is NOT proof it currently
  drops, can be purchased, can be crafted, or can be obtained by Alliance.
- The item-cache baseline predates launch (October 21, 2026).
- New recipe input lists come from a dated Forever client recipe export
  (October 31, 2026), not necessarily current trainer inventories.
- Named bosses are client-reference associations, not verified loot rates.
- Vendor and quest labels rely on a limited set of player-contributed
  observations; check the live NPC, required reputation and quest eligibility.
- "Required character level", "recipe learn minimum" and crafting skill
  yellow/green/grey thresholds are different facts; never substitute one.

TEST INSTALL (ONLY AFTER YOUR OWN BACKUP)
1. Exit WoW Forever. Save your GetGear folder and GetGear SavedVariables.
2. Replace only _forever_/Interface/AddOns/GetGear from the PRIVATE ZIP.
3. Log in to a test character, reload UI and open /getgear. Verify no freeze.
4. In the detailed guide compare several crafted items' reagents to the
   *current game client* (especially 3480, 8203 and 250518).
5. Check Veteran's Chain Belt (#250513): Stondry Darkhammer ACA plans,
   Blacksmithing 45, four reagents and Bind on Pickup.
6. Check Runed Copper Breastplate (#2864): world-drop plans (#2881),
   Blacksmithing 55, not trainer learned.
7. Check Golemguard Chest (#271098): Plunder loot in Hall of Thanes.
8. On a level-13+ Warrior, confirm Ceremonial Leather Harness (#3313)
   and other leather chest/leg/hand items do NOT appear in default Available
   or Earlier Gear. Enable "Show all valid equipment" and confirm they
   become available for manual browsing.
9. Check Warrior/Paladin Mail under 40 and Plate at 40; Hunter/Shaman
   Leather under 40 and Mail at 40. Test Preview around level 39/40.
10. Re-check nine classes, levels 1–60, Preview, Earlier Gear, crafting
   visibility, profession-recipe knowledge, materials and map navigation.
11. If the source, drops, maps or levels disagree with the live client,
   capture item ID, source NPC or quest ID, tooltip and character faction.
12. Report any in-game Lua error and what action preceded it.

HISTORICAL GATE: In the private test stage we did not merge or publish.
Beta 7 is now being published as a public *pre-release*. Do not claim complete
live source validation on the strength of automated tests alone.
