GetGear v0.14.0 Beta 4 — PRIVATE TEST BUILD
============================================
Public v0.12.14 remains unchanged. This is a major architecture change
and has not yet been validated by an actual WoW Forever client.

BEFORE INSTALLING
1. Exit WoW completely and back up your existing addon/SavedVariables if
   you want a rollback. Beta 4 intentionally resets acquired/skipped/hidden
   progress and display preferences once; it does NOT try to migrate them.
2. Replace _forever_/Interface/AddOns/GetGear with this ZIP's GetGear folder.
3. Log in, type /getgear, and check that login remains responsive.

FOCUSED TESTS
1. At level 15 on a Warrior, Handstitched Leather Vest and Pants and
   Copper Dagger should be in Earlier Gear, not Available Now.
   Red Linen Robe (+Intellect cloth) should not be a normal Warrior
   recommendation at all.
2. Check Thick War Axe and Runed Copper Pants have accurate facts and
   remain findable even when aged to Earlier Gear. Seal of Wrynn must not
   appear at level 16 merely because its equip requirement is low.
3. Check the five-level boundary: level 10 is earlier at character 15,
   level 11 is available; Upcoming covers character level +1 to +5.
4. The manual spec selector and /getgear spec feature are REMOVED.
   Preview Guide shows class/level choices only (all nine classes, 1–60).
5. For Mage with a CLEAR dominant Fire/Frost/Arcane talent tree, exclusive
   damage from another school should be suppressed, but Intellect,
   generic spell power, healing and mixed-stat items remain available.
   With ambiguous talents, avoid school-only exclusions.
6. Test Show all valid equipment: older/off-role/novelty options return;
   QA developer/test items never return.
7. Verify profession materials, craftability toggle, bags, Alt Inventory,
   Earlier/Available/Upcoming/Completed/Hidden categories, map buttons,
   pagination, and no freeze on login/reload.
8. Report exact item IDs and tooltip evidence for any live-source mistakes.

The catalogue is NOT a claim that all cache items are currently obtainable.
Archive any in-game Lua errors and report them with the affected action.
