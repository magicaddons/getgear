## Distinctions that must survive

- **Fiery Plate Gauntlets:** [quest 5124](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5124) explicitly lists **both plans 12699 and finished gauntlets 12631** as guaranteed reward entries. This separate published direct-gear alternative is displayed; crafting is not presented as the only route. Original crafted catalogue/ingredients/class facts remain intact. Current quest access remains unverified.
- **Imperial Plate deliveries:** exact quest objectives list Thorium Bar costs of 20 (Shoulders/Belt/Bracers), 40 (Boots), 50 (Helm), 60 (Chest/Leggings). These are quest costs, not recipe reagent replacements. Derotain Mudsipper's published NPC map is in Tanaris; no historical cost is copied across slots.
- **Item-started quests:** Enchanted Thorium Platemail items 18769/18770/18771 begin quests 7649/7650/7651, respectively; their tooltips require level 50 and Blacksmithing 300. These starting books differ from the reward plans 12727/12726/12725. Exact created-by binding spells identify the corresponding top/bottom halves 18779–18784. Six exact half-item pages supply named enemy/zone references, retained as publication-level, historical-capable loot. Blackrock Spire's shared zone ID is not expanded into an assumed upper/lower wing.
- **Sulfuron Hammer plans:** contract item 18628 begins [A Binding Contract 7604](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=7604), which lists contract plus Sulfuron Ingot as delivery objectives and Lokhtos Darkbargainer in Blackrock Depths as turn-in. The contract requires level 60. Its creation spell does not prove a current NPC dialog or live way to acquire the contract; that remains explicit. Plans are not the finished Sulfuras legendary.
- **Wild Leather:** five book pages retain the distinct Alliance Pratt McGrubben and Horde Jangdor Swiftstrider quest IDs in Feralas, not a silently merged faction route.
- **Blue Suede Armor:** [Leather Technique 92817](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=92817) offers a **choice** between the Blue Suede Armor pattern 252883 and Mystic Thick Armor Kit pattern 252833. Published objectives request a Barbaric Harness and Friendly Gelkis Clan Centaur. Narrative directs delivery to Talra in Gelkis Village, Desolace, but numeric giver/turn-in IDs and a precise pin are unestablished. The guide supplies the usable narrative lead with that limitation; no guessed NPC ID or coordinate.
- **Sweet Serenity:** 5305 names Lilith the Lithe; duplicate 8869 has no named actor metadata. The latter is retained as an unresolved duplicate route rather than assigned Lilith by analogy.
- **Access metadata:** four quest pages (2758, 2762, 2763, 5127) list Warrior/Paladin classes. These are published quest access fields, not independently verified live restrictions and not new gear class filters. Minimum quest levels, profession/specialisation access and full prerequisites remain distinct from equip gates and learning skill. The pages can retain inherited historical content.
