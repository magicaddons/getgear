# GetGear — Comprehensive Catalogue Verification and Reconciliation

## 1. Primary objective

Perform an exhaustive, evidence-driven audit of the entire GetGear catalogue against the current live version of WoW Forever.

This is not simply a task to resolve previously identified discrepancies. The objective is to establish, to the greatest extent reasonably possible, that GetGear's catalogue is:

- Complete.
- Factually accurate.
- Current.
- Internally consistent.
- Appropriately sourced.
- Suitable for reliable equipment recommendations.

Do not assume existing catalogue entries are correct simply because they have passed previous automated validation.

Equally, do not assume that an item does not exist simply because it is absent from GetGear or a particular external database.

## 2. Preserve existing work

Begin by reviewing the completed catalogue research task, including its datasets, evidence, reports, validated corrections and unresolved discrepancies.

Reuse reliable research rather than unnecessarily repeating it.

Work from the latest integrated GetGear source, preserving all previously accepted functionality, fixes and improvements.

Do not overwrite validated corrections with less reliable information.

## 3. Resolve outstanding research discrepancies

Investigate and reconcile all outstanding issues from the previous research task, including:

- 92 missing equipment responses.
- 32 unmatched crafting joins.
- 54 commerce skill disagreements.
- The identified enchantment contradiction.
- Outstanding acquisition coordinate disputes.
- Any additional unresolved issues documented in previous research reports.

Determine the underlying cause of each discrepancy rather than merely suppressing or excluding the affected record.

Where definitive resolution is impossible, retain the discrepancy in a structured unresolved-issues register.

## 4. Independently establish catalogue completeness

Do not restrict the investigation to items already present in GetGear.

Construct independent reference inventories of relevant WoW Forever equipment, recipes and acquisition sources using reliable evidence.

Compare these inventories against GetGear to identify:

- Missing equipment.
- Missing recipes.
- Missing acquisition methods.
- Missing quest rewards.
- Missing dungeon drops.
- Missing vendor items.
- Missing crafted items.
- Incorrectly excluded items.
- Duplicate records.
- Obsolete or removed items.
- Classic WoW records that are no longer accurate in Forever.
- Forever-specific items or modifications that have not been incorporated.

Perform reconciliation in both directions: external evidence against GetGear, and GetGear against external evidence.

Cover the full supported level range and all equipment categories and classes, rather than focusing on currently reported examples.

## 5. Verify individual catalogue records

Where applicable, verify every catalogue record against the following attributes:

**Item identity and properties**

- Item ID.
- Item name.
- Required character level.
- Item level.
- Quality.
- Equipment slot.
- Armour or weapon classification.
- Armour value.
- Primary and secondary statistics.
- Resistances.
- Weapon damage and speed.
- Equip, use and proc effects.
- Binding type.
- Class, race, faction and reputation restrictions.
- Other equip or acquisition requirements.

**Acquisition information**

- Quest rewards and prerequisite quest chains.
- Dungeon and raid drops.
- Specific boss or trash-mob sources.
- Open-world drops.
- Rare creatures.
- Vendors.
- Reputation requirements.
- PvP acquisition.
- Crafted equipment.
- Alternative acquisition methods.
- Relevant currency and purchase requirements.

**Crafting information**

- Correct profession.
- Required profession skill.
- Recipe identity.
- Complete and accurate material requirements.
- Material quantities.
- Recipe acquisition method.
- Trainer, vendor or drop source.
- Specialisation requirements.
- Recipe binding restrictions.

**Location information**

- Correct NPC identity.
- Correct zone and subzone.
- Accurate coordinates.
- Correct map identification.
- Dungeon entrance locations.
- Faction accessibility.
- Distinction between exact, approximate and unresolved locations.

Do not manufacture precision where evidence supports only an approximate location.

## 6. Source quality and research restrictions

Prioritise authoritative, independently verifiable, current WoW Forever information.

Where available, prioritise official information, actual live-game evidence and reliable Forever-specific datasets over generic Classic WoW information.

Classic WoW information must not automatically be treated as authoritative for Forever.

Where sources conflict, investigate their provenance, freshness, version applicability and reliability.

Multiple websites reproducing the same underlying dataset do not constitute independent corroboration.

## 7. Evidence and verification methodology

Create a machine-readable verification ledger covering the entire catalogue.

Track verification at both the individual-record and individual-field level.

Distinguish between:

- Directly verified information.
- Reliably corroborated information.
- Partially verified information.
- Legacy or unverified information.
- Contradictory information.
- Missing information.
- Confirmed incorrect information.

An item must not be classified as comprehensively verified merely because its name and item ID have been confirmed.

Likewise, an item with verified statistics but unresolved acquisition information must remain appropriately qualified.

For each correction, retain:

- Original value.
- Corrected value.
- Supporting evidence.
- Source provenance.
- Reason for the change.
- Verification status.

Never silently replace uncertain information with assumptions.

## 8. Systemic correction requirement

Treat every identified discrepancy as a potential systemic issue.

For example, discovering an incorrect profession requirement on one item must trigger an investigation into whether the same underlying data or logic problem affects other recipes, professions or items.

Apply generic, data-driven corrections wherever possible.

Do not introduce isolated item-specific workarounds where the underlying issue can be corrected comprehensively.

Investigate analogous cases across all relevant:

- Classes.
- Equipment categories.
- Level ranges.
- Professions.
- Acquisition methods.
- Zones.
- Faction restrictions.
- Recommendation and presentation views.

GetGear currently prioritises Alliance support. Preserve that scope without incorrectly treating Horde-exclusive equipment as practically obtainable by Alliance characters.

## 9. Implementation and catalogue reconciliation

Incorporate all sufficiently supported corrections into the actual GetGear runtime catalogue.

Do not limit delivery to a research report.

Ensure corrections propagate consistently through:

- Compact recommendation overlay.
- Detailed equipment guide.
- Preview mode.
- Equipment comparisons.
- Crafting material requirements.
- Acquisition descriptions.
- Map markers.
- Recommendation eligibility and filtering.
- Other functionality dependent on catalogue data.

Preserve the distinction between factual equipment eligibility and practical acquisition suitability.

Preserve GetGear's existing philosophy of presenting useful information without unnecessarily dictating player choices.

## 10. Work in persistent, resumable stages

Given the scale of this investigation, divide the work into manageable stages:

**Stage 1 — Baseline and inventory**

Establish the current catalogue, previous research findings, source register and verification framework.

**Stage 2 — Outstanding discrepancies**

Investigate and reconcile the known unresolved research findings.

**Stage 3 — Completeness audit**

Establish independent reference inventories and identify missing, obsolete or duplicate records.

**Stage 4 — Record-level verification**

Audit individual items, statistics, restrictions and effects.

**Stage 5 — Acquisition and crafting verification**

Audit all acquisition methods, recipes, profession requirements and materials.

**Stage 6 — Geographic verification**

Reconcile NPC, vendor, trainer, dungeon and other acquisition locations.

**Stage 7 — Implementation**

Incorporate validated corrections and additions into GetGear.

**Stage 8 — Final validation**

Perform comprehensive regression testing, coverage assessment and final reporting.

Persist research findings, raw evidence, intermediate datasets, completed stages and outstanding work throughout the task.

Maintain a checkpoint document sufficient for another Codex session to resume the work without restarting the investigation.

Do not allow context-window exhaustion, usage limits or interrupted sessions to destroy accumulated research.

## 14. Completion standard

The objective is not simply to produce a cleaner catalogue or resolve a predetermined number of discrepancies.

The objective is to establish the most complete, accurate, independently verifiable GetGear catalogue that can reasonably be constructed using available trustworthy evidence.

Where complete verification is impossible, explicitly identify:

- What remains unknown.
- Why it remains unknown.
- What has already been attempted.
- What evidence would resolve the issue.
- Whether live-game investigation is required.

Continue investigating reasonable avenues for resolution rather than prematurely treating unresolved discrepancies as unavoidable.

The final result must provide a defensible understanding of both the catalogue's accuracy and the limits of that accuracy.

Proceed autonomously through the research and implementation stages wherever possible, maintaining persistent checkpoints and respecting all existing source restrictions, development requirements and publication authorisation boundaries.
