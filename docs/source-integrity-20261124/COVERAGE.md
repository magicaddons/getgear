Class memberships overlap; they are not independent item counts. The complete
class/slot counts are in `summary.json`. All stored levels are inventoried; new
service/practicality regression fixtures cover levels 1–60 across all nine class
contexts and every stored slot label. Original faction-valid combinations are
also exercised by the retained faction suites; an Alliance Shaman preview is
not a claim of a supported native Alliance Shaman character.

The 7,423 equipment route decisions include 170 fresh exact guide joins, 3,115
retained historical approved-publication references, 360 unapproved-evidence
holds and 3,778 routes without traceable route-level evidence in the inspected
projection. These counts do not include the 27 raw bag quest routes; bags have a
separate complete capacity/source qualification ledger. Historical approved
publication references were provenance-reviewed, **not all freshly re-fetched**.

Every unresolved record remains in the CSV/register with a status and scope.
The 92 failures are retained with the exact HTTP/error outcome. Requests were
not repeatedly retried to manufacture success. Remaining research requires exact
approved current evidence or applicable native-client observations; structural
test success does not remove these research gaps.

outcome, corrected title, namespace discrepancy and published map. Three repeated
